# When Execution Becomes Boundless: Organizational Form in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**当执行趋于无限：人工智能时代的组织形态**

*Wang Zexin · v1.0 · October 2026 · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0*

---

## Abstract

Cognitive execution is being commoditized as physical strength and information processing were before it. This paper asks a narrower question: when execution becomes effectively free, what remains for an organization to allocate?

The answer offered here is a design claim rather than a finding. Organizations should treat judgment as the object of allocation, and should attach every autonomous action chain to a subject that can be named. On that basis the paper sets out five design principles, a balance sheet of organizational boundaries, and three institutional devices.

Each principle rests on a premise the paper chooses to bear: the visibility of execution states, the atrophy of unused judgment, the location of stability in rules rather than in charts, named anchoring at either the chain or the function level, and the budgetability of scarce judgment. The premises are stated one by one, together with the range within which each principle fails if its premise does not hold.

Seventeen propositions are advanced: six with explicit test designs, four restatements of the model, and seven conjectures.

**Keywords:** artificial intelligence; organizational theory; judgment rights; inalienability; organizational boundary; anchored accountability

## Five design principles

- **R1 Holographic visibility.** Judgment presupposes visibility of the execution state. Layers whose function is to compress information stage by stage therefore lose their reason to exist.
- **R2 Symbiosis.** Humans and agents are mutually conditioned. Net extraction by either side must be measured and corrected; unused judgment atrophies.
- **R3 Fluidity.** Structure is recomputed per task. Stability belongs in the rule layer, not in a standing chart of posts.
- **R4 Anchoring.** No action without an anchor. Every autonomous exercise falls within the authorization of a named subject — an individual in the chain, or a post carrying that business function.
- **R5 Regeneration.** Judgment is scarce. It requires a budget, stock monitoring, and a mechanism for replenishment.
